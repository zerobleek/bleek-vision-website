# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three overlapping audiences arrive at bleekvision.com:

- **Prospective users** browsing for an iPhone/iPad app — they land from an App Store listing, a search, or a share, and want to quickly judge whether a given app (MadWeather, FOCUS//DECK, UNCONTAINED, MomKnows!, Cipher Protocol) is for them and tap through to the store.
- **The curious** who want to know who is behind these apps — checking the studio is real, run by a real person, and worth trusting with a purchase or their data.
- **Industry / press / collaborators** sizing up the studio: a recruiter, a fellow developer, a writer, a potential partner forming a fast impression of the work and the taste behind it.

Context of use: short, evaluative visits, often on a phone, often one app deep. The job to be done is "show me the work, prove it's legit, let me act." Success is a tap to an App Store listing or a launch-notify signup, and a lasting impression that this is a studio with real craft.

## Product Purpose

The single public home for Bleek Vision LLC — an independent iOS development studio run by Shabaka Malik Banks. It exists to present the apps as a coherent body of work, establish the studio's credibility and point of view, and route visitors to the App Store (or launch waitlists for unreleased apps). It is not a sales funnel or a lead machine; it is a portfolio and a statement of identity. Success looks like: visitors understand what each app is in seconds, trust the studio behind it, and act — without ever feeling marketed at.

## Brand Personality

**Crafted and precise** above all — every detail is intentional, engineered, and held to a high bar; the site itself is proof of the care that goes into the apps ("one dev, one laptop, one idea at a time"). The voice is confident and has a point of view, plain-spoken and specific rather than hyped. Independent and human: a real founder, no corporate gloss, no faceless "we." Quietly characterful where it earns it (per-app color worlds, named mascots, borough personalities) without ever tipping into loud.

Three words: **crafted, precise, confident.**

## Anti-references

The site must not look or feel like any of these:

- **Generic SaaS / AI-landing-page template** — tiny tracked uppercase eyebrow above every section, hero-metric stat blocks, identical icon-heading-text card grids, gradient text, numbered section markers as scaffolding. The "AI made that" look.
- **Corporate / agency gloss** — faceless enterprise tone, stock-photo teams, the empower / streamline / seamless / world-class buzzword family. No "we" hiding a solo founder.
- **Loud / hype marketing** — fake urgency, countdowns, "revolutionary / game-changing," aggressive CTAs, growth-hack chrome. No false feature claims (e.g. don't claim "no ads" where an app has ads).

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the portfolio piece.** Its own craft is the argument for the studio's craft. Anything sloppy undercuts the entire pitch.
2. **Show the work, don't sell it.** Let the apps and their screenshots carry the page; copy is specific and honest, never promotional. Route to the store, don't pressure.
3. **One identity, many worlds.** A single restrained studio shell (navy / silver / one periwinkle accent) holds distinct per-product color worlds inside the cards. Brand cohesion outside, app personality inside.
4. **Specific over aphoristic.** Real detail ("72 levels, nine atom types"; "borough personalities") beats punchy slogans. Concrete nouns and verbs, never marketing abstraction.
5. **Honest and human.** A real founder, real constraints, no corporate veneer and no claims the apps can't back up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its background (watch the muted-gray-on-navy and the light MomKnows card world); large text ≥3:1. Keep the existing `:focus-visible` rings and the `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (the site has heavy ambient motion — aurora, pulsing dots, wordmark animation — that must fully quiet down). Maintain semantic landmarks (nav / section / footer), descriptive alt text on every product shot, and keyboard operability for the nav toggle and all CTAs.
